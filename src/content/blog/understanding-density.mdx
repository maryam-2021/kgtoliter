## Why Mass and Volume Are Not Interchangeable

Kilograms (**kg**) measure mass, while litres (**L**) measure spatial volume. To bridge the two, you need the material's **density** (*D*), measured in kg/L.

### The Conversion Formula

- **Volume (L)** = Mass (kg) / Density (kg/L)
- **Mass (kg)** = Volume (L) * Density (kg/L)

### Standard Reference Densities

- **Water (4°C)**: 1.000 kg/L
- **Milk**: 1.030 kg/L
- **Diesel**: 0.832 kg/L
- **Olive Oil**: 0.918 kg/L
